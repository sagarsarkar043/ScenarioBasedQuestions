# Customer Acquisition Trend

## Business Context

Marketing team wants to track growth in new customers.

## Business Problem

How many new customers were acquired this month?

## Data Available

1. CustomerID
2. SignupDate
3. MarketingChannel

## Step-by-Step Approach

Step 1: Filter signups in current month
Step 2: Count distinct customers
Step 3: Compare with previous month
Step 4: Break down by channel

## Technical Solution

Use date filtering. Group by marketing channel

## Business Insight

Identify most effective acquisition channel.
