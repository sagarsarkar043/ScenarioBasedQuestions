# How to Understand Any Scenario Question (Framework)

Whenever you get a scenario, break it into 5 steps:

## Step 1: Understand the Business Problem

Ask:

1. What is the actual issue?
2. Is it revenue, cost, customer, fraud, churn, efficiency?

## Step 2: Identify Stakeholders

1. Who cares about this?
2. CEO? Marketing? Risk team? Operations?

## Step 3: Define KPIs

1. What metrics matter?
2. Revenue, Conversion Rate, CLV, Default Rate, Fraud %, etc.

## Step 4: Data Needed

1. What tables?
2. What columns?
3. Historical or real-time?

## Step 5: Analytical Approach

1. Descriptive?
2. Diagnostic?
3. Predictive?
4. Prescriptive?

# Structure to Answer Scenario-Based Questions

Use this answer structure in interviews:

## 1. Clarify the problem
## 2. Define KPIs
## 3. Identify data sources
## 4. Perform analysis
## 5. Share insights
## 6. Recommend actions
